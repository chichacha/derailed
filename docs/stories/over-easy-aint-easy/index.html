02 / Breakfast taxonomy

What's the difference?

☀️ Sunny-side up

Fried on one side only. Whites set, yolk visible and runny. No flip.

🍳 Over easy

Flipped briefly to set the second side. Yolk stays runny.

🥚 Over medium

Flipped and cooked a bit longer. Yolk becomes partially set.

💪 Over hard

Flipped and cooked until the yolk is fully set.

Other breakfast egg orders: scrambled, poached, soft-/hard-boiled, omelette, basted, and more. Matter.js models rigid bodies and collisions; the flipping motion and splattered yolk here are intentionally exaggerated cartoon approximations, not a fluid/food science model.
