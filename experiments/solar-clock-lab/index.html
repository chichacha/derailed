Why the darkest morning comes later

Latitude changes daylight duration; longitude changes solar noon relative to a meridian. Earth's tilt and orbital eccentricity cause the equation of time: solar noon drifts relative to a uniform clock. Around the northern winter solstice, that drift can delay sunrise even while daylight duration increases.
