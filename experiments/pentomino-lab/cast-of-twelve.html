The Cast of Twelve.

Twelve shapes, twelve personalities. Geometry gives each pentomino its character; colour and pattern give it a costume. The puzzle stays mathematically honest while the artwork takes a detour.

Meet the hero: X

FIVE SQUARES · FIVE FEATURE COLOURS
Tap a square to cycle its colour · all five stay unique
SPECIAL DESIGN PERMISSION

The Center Uncle ✳

真ん中おじさん

X has only one free geometric orientation. Its perfectly symmetric silhouette makes it the ideal anchor for this tiny cast.

Rule X01 — One colour per square. Use all five feature colours exactly once. The centre is the anchor; four colourful arms surround it. No other piece receives all five colours.

The supporting cast

ELEVEN PIECES · ELEVEN DISTINCT MOTIF DIRECTIONS

A design grammar, not a prison.

Eleven supporting pieces each use one dominant feature colour and a restrained neutral accent. Their named motif is a preferred visual language, not an absolute rule. X alone may display all five feature colours simultaneously. These are concept sketches for artwork, not manufacturing-ready vector cut paths. Future versions can make the rules editable, synchronize them with the main Pattern Lab, and export true fabrication outlines.
