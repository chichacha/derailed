The Center Uncle ✳

真ん中おじさん

X has only one free geometric orientation. Its perfectly symmetric silhouette makes it the ideal anchor for this tiny cast.

Rule X01 — One colour per square. Use all five feature colours exactly once. The centre is the anchor; four colourful arms surround it. No other piece receives all five colours.

The supporting cast

11 CHARACTERS · POSTURES FROM GOMASU DATA

A design grammar, not a prison.

Every composition uses one visual family — linear, curved, split, or blocky. Character-specific accents follow the same five square identities through rotation and reflection. Each card cycles through its actual Gomasu posture IDs, including front and back where distinct. X remains the five-colour exception. These are editable concept sketches, not fabrication-ready cutting paths.
