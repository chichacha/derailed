Twelve characters. Sixty-three distinct postures. Turn, reflect and dress every shape in one shared visual language. The geometry comes from Gomasu; the graphic treatment is an experiment.

All orientations

63 postures
Why 63? Every pentomino is five squares, but not every shape has the same symmetries. X has one orientation; I has two; T, U, V, W and Z have four each; F, L, N, P and Y have eight each. Front and back are meaningful only when reflection creates a new shape. The posture IDs, metadata, and character names come from the Gomasu source datasets.
