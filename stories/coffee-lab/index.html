03 / Three useful things to know

A little knowledge. A lot more coffee.

01 / NOT JUST MILK

Texture changes the drink.

A cappuccino usually has a more aerated, foamy texture. A flat white is known for fine, silky microfoam. A latte is generally larger and milkier. The foam slider is a visual simplification, not a separate ingredient to measure.

02 / TWO MACCHIATOS

Which one gets marked?

An espresso macchiato is espresso “marked” with a little milk or foam. A latte macchiato starts with milk and gets “marked” with espresso. Similar words, very different proportions—and pour order matters.

03 / HOME BARISTA RULE

Choose your cup first.

For a small gathering, make one espresso recipe repeatably, then vary cup size and milk texture. The numbers here teach comparison; your machine, beans, pitcher, and taste decide the final recipe.

For more context: Breville's latte / cappuccino / flat white comparison and the SCA barista drinks comparison (PDF). Recipe conventions vary by café and region.
